Perlin noise

Each channel adds signed Perlin noise (−amplitude … +amplitude) to one part and axis. Octaves add finer detail, each at twice the frequency and half the size. Coord × is the multiplier on the noise's coordinate (time in seconds × Coord × All ×): larger values make the noise change more quickly, smaller ones more slowly. 1 is about one swing a second; 0.3 about one every three seconds. Changing it while running does not make the noise jump. All three dolls get the same noise.
