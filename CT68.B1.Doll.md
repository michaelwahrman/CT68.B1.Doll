# CT68.B1.Doll

Three MIDI-controlled caroler dolls, made from the photographs in `C:\Users\micha\Downloads\claude\images\darkride\` (`caroler 2.webp`, `caroler 3.webp`, and a doll from `caroler 1`). They work in a browser (three.js / WebGL) and in Arnold (`.ass` for `kick`). All three answer the same controls and MIDI CCs and move together (ganged).

B1 is a copy of CT68.A7.Doll, which is unchanged. B1 only adds two notices to the page: see "Changes from CT68.A7" below. The rest of this file is A7's.

A7 is a copy of CT68.A6.Doll, which is unchanged. Everything from A1 to A6 still works. A7 moves the dolls further from the camera, adds a candle blow-out and a flame level, and makes the dolls easy to put in another three.js page, the Darkride.

## What A7 changes

1. **"The starting position should have the dolls somewhat further from the camera, perhaps at least another third. In this way, they look more doll like."**
   - The start view is 1.6 times the distance that just fits the three dolls in (A5 and A6: 1.15). That is about 40% further away, a bit more than the third asked for.
   - The angle is unchanged: from the front left, a little above, the green doll nearest.
   - The fog now moves out with the start distance, so the dolls are not greyed by it.
   - **V** or **Start view** still goes back to this view.
2. **"There should be a button that causes the flames to flicker, then go out, putting the dolls in darkness. Then after a few seconds, the flames can reignite and return to their normal illumination."**
   - **Blow out candles** (key **O**, MIDI CC 38) runs the sequence:
     - **Flicker, about 1.6 s:** the flames gutter harder and harder, leaning and stretching.
     - **Going out, about 0.45 s:** the flames die down.
     - **In the dark, 4 s by default:** the room lights go down to 4%, so the dolls stand almost in black.
     - **Lighting again, about 1.3 s:** each flame catches, sputters, flares a little, and settles to normal.
   - Each doll's candle goes out and lights again a little after the one before (0.22 s), so they do not move in lockstep.
   - **Dark for … s** sets how long the dark lasts (0 to 60 s). **Light now** ends the dark at once.
   - The room's lights (the hemisphere, the key spot and the rim) dim and flicker with the candles.
   - The line under the buttons says what is happening: lit, flickering, going out, in the dark, lighting again.
   - The blow-out runs on the animation clock, so it records smoothly in "every frame" mode.
3. **"There should be a slider that allows the user to increase or decrease the illumination / size of the flames."**
   - **Flame level**, from 0 to 3, with 1 as normal (key **[** steps it down by 0.25, **]** steps it up; MIDI CC 37).
   - The light from each candle grows in proportion to the level, and its reach grows with the square root of the level.
   - The flame's size grows with the square root of the level, so at 3 the flame is about 1.7 times as big. At 0 the flames and their light are gone.
   - The glow of the wax follows the level.
   - **Export:** **Download .ass** writes the candle lights at the flame level, and leaves out the flames and the candle lights while the candles are out. **Save pose .json** stores `flameLevel`, and `doll_export.mjs` reads it.
4. **"The three dolls will be included in another web browser, the Darkride, so whatever can be prepared to make that easy would be good."**
   - **`embed/ct68_carolers.js`:** the dolls as one script for any three.js page.
     - It is a classic `<script>`, like every file of the Darkride. It brings no three.js of its own: it uses the page's (`window.THREE`, or one you pass in).
     - The textures are packed in, so it works from the disk with no server.
     - `const carolers = await CT68Carolers.create(THREE, { roomLights: [yourLights] })`, then `scene.add(carolers.group)`, then `carolers.update(seconds)` every frame.
   - **The API:**
     - **The pose:** `setPose`.
     - **The performance:** `sing`, `talk`, `spin360`, `blink`.
     - **The candles:** `blowOut`, `relight`, `setFlameLevel`, `roomLight`.
     - **MIDI:** `handleCC` and `handleMIDI` with the dolls' own CC map, or call the actions from the host's own MIDI layer.
     - **The rest:** the hair, the noise, events and `dispose`.
   - **`embed/example.html`:** a host scene of its own (a floor, its own lights, a box) with the dolls added, scaled and placed, with buttons and MIDI.
   - **`EMBED.md`:** how to put the dolls in the Darkride, with the whole API.
   - **`src/carolers.js`:** the same code as an ES module. The page uses it too. The candles, the singing and talking, the blinks, the noise and the MIDI defaults were moved into it from the page, so the page and the embed run the same code.
5. **Also:**
   - **`index.html`** is built next to `dolls_midi.html` (the same page), for onrender and other web hosts.
   - **A fix:** a page opened in a hidden tab or a window of no size made the camera's position NaN for good, so nothing was drawn. It now waits for a real size. This bug was in A5 and A6 too.
   - **The label:** the version label and the tab title read **CT68.A7.Doll**. As in A6, the version is set once, in `VERSION` near the end of `src/doll_rig.js`.

The browser keeps its saved settings under new A7 keys. Your MIDI map and noise channels carry over from A6 (or A5), and the hair starts at the A6 default.

## What's on the page

- **The three dolls, left to right:** Green velvet (caroler 2), Cream lace (caroler 3), Blonde patchwork (caroler 1).
- **Controls:** one slider per control, ganged. **Reset pose** and **Demo motion** (a gentle, slow motion).
- **Perform:**
  - **Sing** (on at start) and **Talk**: the mouths form the words of a short carol or a short speech.
  - **Spin 360°**.
  - **Blink**, and **Auto blink** (very occasional: every 8 to 20 s).
- **Candles (A7):** **Blow out candles**, **Light now**, **Dark for … s**, and the **Flame level** slider.
- **Perlin noise:** channels of signed Perlin noise. Each channel has:
  - a part and an axis;
  - an amplitude in degrees, octaves, and a coordinate multiplier.

  **All ×** speeds every channel up or slows it down.
- **Hair:** styles, thickness and curl opacity. The top of the head is always solid (A6).
- **Record video:**
  - An H.264 .mp4 of the dolls' part of the view, at 1280, 1920 or screen width.
  - Two timings: **every frame** (smooth: the clock steps 1/30 s a frame) or **real time**.
- **Textures:** the doll textures, or the UV test texture.
- **MIDI:**
  - A device list, a channel filter and the last CC received.
  - The map, with **Learn**.
  - **Default map** puts the defaults below back.
- **Export:** **Download .ass** of this pose, and **Save pose .json**.
- **The label** (top left) **CT68.B1.Doll**, the key help, and a frame-rate readout.
- **The notices** (bottom of the picture, B1): no audio, and the copyright.
- **The panel** is shown at start. **H** or **Hide ✕** hides it, and **☰ Controls** brings it back.

## MIDI map (default)

All channels are accepted by default. A CC value of 0–127 is spread across the control's range. A "button" CC presses when it goes from below 64 to 64 or more.

| CC | Control | 0 → 127 |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | Head turn | −70° (her right) → +70° (her left) |
| 21 | Head nod | −25° (down) → +25° (up) |
| 22 | Raise candle arm | 0 (at her waist) → 1 (held high) |
| 23 | Eyes left/right | −1 → +1 (±30°) |
| 24 | Eyes up/down | −1 → +1 (±20°) |
| 25 | Mouth open | 0 → 1 |
| 26 | Mouth shape | −1 (rounded "oo") → +1 (wide "ee") |
| 27 | Body sway | −1 → +1 (±6°) |
| 28 | Spin | 0° → 360° |
| 32 | Eyelids | 0 (open) → 1 (closed) |
| 37 | **Flame level (A7)** | 0 → 3 (1 is normal) |
| 29 | Sing (on/off) | button |
| 30 | Spin 360° | button |
| 31 | Record (on/off) | button (the page only) |
| 33 | Blink | button |
| 34 | Auto blink (on/off) | button |
| 35 | Talk (on/off) | button |
| 36 | Noise (on/off) | button |
| 38 | **Blow out candles (A7)** | button |

The pose controls are in `CONTROLS` in `src/doll_rig.js`. The buttons and the flame level are in `TRIGGERS` and `LEVELS` in `src/carolers.js`.

## How it is built

- **`src/doll_rig.js`** (A1 on) is the rig: one builder makes every doll from its `doll.json` and textures. It has the head, the face and mouth, the eyes and lids, the hair, the bonnet, the arms and hands, and the candle. It is shared by the page, the embed and the Node exporter, and imports nothing: THREE is passed in.
- **`src/carolers.js`** (A7) holds what the page and an embedding page share:
  - **`Candles`:** the candle lights, the flame level and the blow-out.
  - **`Performer`:** sing, talk, spin and blinks.
  - **`perlin1`, `fbm` and `NoiseMixer`:** the noise.
  - **The MIDI defaults.**
  - **`createCarolers()`:** all of it in one group.
- **`src/viewer.html`** is the page. `build.mjs` packs three.js r180 (`lib/`), the rig, `carolers.js`, the exporter, the recorder and every texture (as data URLs) into it, so `index.html` works offline from the disk.
- **`src/ass_export.js`** writes the posed dolls as an Arnold `.ass` (and OBJ). **`doll_export.mjs`** does it from Node for every pose in `poses/`.
- **`src/mp4_record.js`** is the .mp4 recorder (WebCodecs, H.264).
- **`tools/doll_textures.py`** (run it in WSL) turns each `dolls/<name>/spec.json` (a photograph, a prompt and its measurements) into the doll's `tex/` and `doll.json`. That is how a new doll is made without new code.
- **`embed/ct68_carolers.js`** (A7, built) is the rig and `carolers.js` as one classic script, with the dolls' data packed in. **`embed/example.html`** is a host page.

## Known limits

- With the candles out, the room is at 4%, not black, so the dolls' outlines can just be made out. `BLOWOUT.darkness` in `src/carolers.js` sets it.
- The flame level changes only the candles' light, not the room's key light. The flames light the dolls up close, and the room light stays as it was.
- The embed is about 1.6 MB, nearly all of it textures.
